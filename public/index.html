GROW AS YOU GO: A Gamified study tracker app

Grow as You Go (GYG), a gamified study tracker app that combines a task organizer, Pomodoro timer, and virtual gardening reward system. It covers the app’s core features: adding and tracking school tasks, time management using the Pomodoro technique, plant growth progression linked to completed work, and unlocking basic botanical facts as rewards.
